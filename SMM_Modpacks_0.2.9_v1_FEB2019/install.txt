TLDR: Copy mod folders into your Scrap Mechanic Mods folder and enable them in-game.


PORTED FOR SCRAP MECHANIC 0.2.9
===============================

This pack was originally built for 0.3.5 and has been adapted for 0.2.9.

What works on 0.2.9:
- Static blocks, wedges, panels, and decorative parts (The Modpack Polygons is the most complete)
- Native interactables: thrusters, pistons, buttons, logic gates, seats, spotlights, etc.
- Custom headlights/spotlights (converted to 0.2.9 inline light settings)
- Sports balls and bowling props (native Havok physics — roll, bounce, and collide; no custom Lua bounce)

What does NOT work on 0.2.9 (removed or static-only):
- Legacy Support mod (removed — not needed for 0.2.9)
- All custom Lua logic parts (math block, radar, wireless, block spawner, wings, etc.)
- BETA weapons/toys (cannons, flamethrower, skull launcher, etc.)


How to install:

Extract the modpack folders to the location of the Scrap Mechanic mod tool folder:

c:\Users\[username]\AppData\Roaming\Axolot Games\Scrap Mechanic\User\User_[numbers]\Mods\


If you do not find a "Mods" folder, you can create one.
It should be right next to the "Blueprints" and "Save" folders.

Like this:
c:\Users\[username]\AppData\Roaming\Axolot Games\Scrap Mechanic\User\User_[numbers]\Mods\The Modpack


Enable/disable individual mods in the in-game Mod Manager (toggle each modpack on or off).


That's it!
